Created by Lode Verschelde

This free tool generates GS1-128 SSCC barcodes from a list of SSCC numbers and calculates SSCC check digits using the GS1 mod-10 algorithm. SSCCs are 18-digit identifiers used in SAP EWM and across logistics for shipping units.
